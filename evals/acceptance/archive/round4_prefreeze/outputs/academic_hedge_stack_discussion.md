The data were collected retrospectively, so recall bias in the exposure measure cannot be ruled out. Residual confounding may explain part of the association, and the sample was relatively small (n = 418), which limits how far the findings can be generalized. Even so, sleep duration was associated with self-rated health (β = −0.34, 95% CI −0.51 to −0.17; p < 0.001), consistent with a dose-response pattern.
