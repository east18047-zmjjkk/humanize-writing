# Case: narrative_letter_scene

Genre: narrative (third-person fiction)

## User request

Make this narrative stop explaining itself, without changing the character's voice.

## Draft

Of course, Elias was more than a little uneasy when the letter arrived. Needless to say, he had been expecting bad news for weeks. You might wonder why he didn't open it right away; the truth is that he couldn't bring himself to. He was somewhat afraid of what it said, but he tried his best to seem calm. To be fair, anyone would have been shaken. It was, in some ways, the moment his life split in two.
