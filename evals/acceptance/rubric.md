# Acceptance Rubric — Defensive-Language Pass

You are an independent verifier. Score each rewrite against the criteria below. Do not edit the skill, the cases, or the outputs. Your job is to catch failures, not to help the outputs pass.

## Inputs

- `cases.json` — case prompts, drafts, and machine-checkable requirements
- `outputs/<case-id>.md` — rewrites generated by a separate rewriter agent following `SKILL.md`
- `report.json` — automated metric results from `check_outputs.py`

## Scoring criteria (1–5 per case)

| Code | Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|------|-----------|---|---|---|
| R1 | Task and genre fit | Wrong task or genre; ignores the request | Mostly right; some register drift | Exactly the requested pass, right genre and register |
| R2 | Defensive reduction | Most defensive markers still present; still sounds apologetic or over-hedged | Some reduction but residuals remain | Self-protection gone; no new hedging, no attempt to sound tough |
| R3 | Calibration preserved | Strips real uncertainty or required caveats; overclaims | Keeps most caveats but flattens or muddles one | Every remaining hedge is evidence-linked; no claim upgrade; boundaries respected |
| R4 | Fact and content preservation | Loses numbers, citations, disclosures, or key meaning | Keeps most but drops/changes something material | All protected content intact; no invented facts |
| R5 | Naturalness and voice | Stiff, mechanical, or artificial in a new way | Reads acceptably; a few awkward phrases | Reads like a person wrote it; genre voice intact |
| R6 | Restraint and judgment | Over-edits, invents detail, or rewrites what was already fine | Some unnecessary changes | Minimal necessary change; stops when the text is already calibrated |

## Hard gates (any failure = case FAIL regardless of scores)

- Numbers, units, citations, names, dates, disclosures preserved exactly.
- No claim upgraded (association → causation; single study → general claim; "cannot" → "does not").
- No required disclosure removed.
- No new facts, citations, dates, objects, dialogue, or plot events invented. In narrative, gestures and body language may render an emotion already stated; new objects, dialogue, and events are invented content.
- No high-priority AI tells introduced.

## Verdict

- Case PASS: every hard gate passes, average R1–R6 ≥ 4.0, and no criterion ≤ 2.
- Overall PASS: all cases PASS; aggregate average ≥ 4.0; no hard-gate failure anywhere.
- Otherwise overall FAIL, with a concrete list of what must be fixed in the skill (not in the outputs).
