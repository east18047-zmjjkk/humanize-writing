Because the data were collected retrospectively, we cannot rule out recall bias in the exposure measure, and part of the association could reflect residual confounding. The sample was small (n = 418), so whether these findings generalize beyond this cohort is still an open question. Even so, sleep duration was associated with self-rated health (β = −0.34, 95% CI −0.51 to −0.17; p < 0.001), a pattern consistent with a dose-response relationship.
