# Example User Style Profile

Formality: conversational-professional  
Rhythm: frequent short sentences (under 12 words) mixed with medium ones; occasional longer explanatory sentence  
Contractions: yes (don't, it's, we've)  
Vocabulary: plain verbs preferred; avoids business jargon and AI tells  
Voice: direct, slightly dry, concrete; mild hedging only when genuinely uncertain  
Papers/academic: more precise wording, fewer contractions, still cuts throat-clearing and empty emphasis  
Stories: concrete actions and details over emotional labeling  

Notes: Prefers endings that stop rather than summarize. Likes one specific example per major point when available.
